Streets, driveways and paths. `buildCorridor(definition)` turns a centreline (lines and tangent circular arcs, stationed by arc length), a cross-section (width, camber or one-sided cross-fall, optional kerbs) and a longitudinal profile (heights or grades at stations, with symmetric parabolic vertical curves of length `L = K·|g2 − g1|` at each grade change) into one mesh sampled from error bounds: the sagitta of arcs at the outermost offset and the chord deviation of each vertical curve stay below the tolerance (default 5 mm). `corridorProblems` lists what keeps a definition from being built (grade above the maximum, vertical curve too short or not fitting, width not positive, stations outside the centreline, radius too large or tighter than half the width, self-intersecting centreline or edges). `addCorridorInStore` writes it as IFC 4.3: an `IfcPavement` with aggregated `IfcKerb`s and a tessellated body, positioned by an `IfcAlignment` with horizontal and vertical layouts (`PARABOLICARC` segments), and records the definition so `readCorridorInStore` reads it back. `fitTinToOutlines` cuts a corridor's outline into a terrain TIN as constrained edges at the corridor's heights and removes what lies inside, so the terrain stays one conforming surface that meets the street with no gap and no step.
