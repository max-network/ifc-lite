Vaults and arched heads for historic buildings and existing-state surveys. `addVaultToStore` writes an `IfcSlab` (`USERDEFINED`, ObjectType `VAULT`) over a rectangle: a barrel vault as one `IfcExtrudedAreaSolid` of its arch band, bounded by an `IfcIndexedPolyCurve` of `IfcArcIndex` arcs (a polyline in IFC2X3), and a cross (groin) vault as a closed `IfcPolygonalFaceSet` (an `IfcFacetedBrep` in IFC2X3) of two perpendicular circular barrels with the same crown, meeting along their exact intersection curves. Arches come from one formula: radius `(s²/4 + f²) / 2f` with its centre `f - r` above the springing (round at `f = s/2`, pointed from two equal circles above it); the extrados is concentric at `r + t`; chords stay within 5 mm. `editVaultInStore` and `readVaultInStore` change and read one through its `IfcLite_Vault` definition and placement, and `vaultPlanLines` gives its plan outline, crown lines and groins from the same geometry. Wall openings, doors and windows take `Head: { Shape: 'round' }` or `{ Shape: 'segmental', Rise }`: the void, the lining, panes, mullions and leaves follow the arch, `readOpeningHead` reads it back from the profile, and `editHostedElementInStore` keeps the head through a resize or changes it with `Head` (`null` makes it rectangular). Retiring a product shape now also removes the faces and point list of a tessellated body.
